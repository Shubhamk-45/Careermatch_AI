About DeepfakeGuard

A hackathon project on Responsible AI, deepfake detection, cyber resilience and digital trust.

How it works
1. Upload – you pick a photo or video; type and size are validated.
2. Analyse – a pre-trained deepfake model (e.g. via Hugging Face Transformers) runs on the file. Videos are sampled into frames.
3. Result – one of three outcomes: Potential manipulation detected, No obvious manipulation detected, or Inconclusive.
4. Stay safe – guidance on verifying the content and reporting it.

Responsible AI: models can produce false positives and false negatives. A confidence score is not a guarantee, and "no manipulation detected" never proves content is real. Files are processed in memory and not kept.

Cyber Awareness Center

Practical steps to verify content and avoid fraud.

Digital Trust Checker
🔗 Trace the source: find the original post or publisher. Reverse-image-search the picture.
🧾 Check metadata: date, device and location can help, but they are easy to edit or strip.
📰 Cross-check: look for the same story on official or established news channels.
📞 Impersonation scams: if a voice or video asks for money, hang up and call back on a known number.
🚩 Report: report to the platform, and in India to cybercrime.gov.in or helpline 1930.

Report suspicious content

If a fake photo, video or voice targets you or someone you know, act in this order.

1. Do not forward it. Sharing spreads the harm.
2. Save evidence: screenshots, the link, username, date and time. Do not delete chats.
3. Report on the platform (Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube, X) using “Report” → impersonation / fake media.
4. India: file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 for financial fraud.
5. Intimate fake images: use StopNCII.org to block them from spreading.
6. Tell someone you trust and never pay or send OTPs to anyone who threatens you with a fake.
